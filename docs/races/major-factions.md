# Major Factions

Certain organizations and powers exert influence across multiple continents and layers of the world. Their goals, methods, and reach vary enormously.

---

## The World Bazaar

The primary force behind trade between the Surface and the Caverns, and a major operator of black markets. They hold a near-monopoly on the use of spatially attuned leylines and are among the few groups that have successfully established trade with dungeons near the core.

They are one of the only organizations on the global no-kill list of the Silent Army.

---

## The Silent Army (Assassin’s Guild)

A shadowy organization feared across the world. Led (at least according to rumor) by Umbra, a cultivator of extraordinary skill in shadow and space.

Their true goals and internal structure are almost completely unknown. What is known is that they are capable of erasing entire regions overnight and that speaking too freely about them is often fatal. Most people follow a simple rule: do not involve yourself with the Silent Army unless you have no other choice.

---

## The Alchemist’s Guild

The largest organized body of Alchemists dedicated to studying and codifying magical phenomena. They are the world’s leading producers of magical items outside of dungeons.

The Guild is not truly unified — individual Alchemists are competitive and secretive. They maintain a network of libraries across the world containing vast amounts of knowledge, much of it valuable, some of it cursed, misleading, or simply wrong. Access to these libraries is extremely restricted.

---

## The Veiled Cartographers

A group dedicated to mapping leylines and the shifting continents. Many members are forcefully attuned to Spatial mana. They maintain a powerful magical map updated by connected field maps, giving them one of the best (though still incomplete) pictures of the world’s layout.

They have a particular interest in the relic believed to lie at the center of the Edge of the World.

---

## The Bankers

The organization responsible for maintaining a stable currency based on Potential. Their founder discovered a method of converting free Potential into coins and crystals of varying grades.

They specialize in Potential transfers and core etching (allowing skills to be transferred along with raw Potential). Officially based in the Quantifier’s Archive and operating under a complex arrangement with the Quantifier itself. A hidden faction within the Bankers is said to be working against the Quantifier.

---

## The Soulbinders

A powerful and secretive group that collects dungeon cores and turns them into soul-bound items or tomes of knowledge. They are also responsible for many of the legacy trials found across the world.

Internal factions exist with differing goals: some seek general advancement of the world through knowledge, others deliberately create extreme trials to forge legendary heroes, and a third group appears interested primarily in breaking people and seeing what results.

---

## The Eternal Wardens

Powerful individuals who have united around the goal of maintaining the balance of power. They position themselves near major threats in order to contain them without becoming threats themselves. The best-known group is the Wardens of Destruction.

When something crosses into what they consider “dangerous territory,” the Wardens tend to respond decisively. This has earned them a fearful reputation. Occasionally they judge the world ready to handle a previously contained threat and release it, usually resulting in widespread destruction and the rise of new legends.

---

## The Dreamweavers

A collection of groups that dwell primarily in the Dream and the Mindscape. They have specialized in traveling through and shaping these unstable regions.

Major internal divisions include Dreamers (who stabilize areas), Voyagers (who specialize in travel), Monks (who aid others and maintain self-control), and Manipulators (who focus on altering minds and existences). They are difficult to find if they do not wish to be found, and their willingness to treat those with weaker minds as expendable has earned them many enemies.

---

## The Shattered Crown

A loose coalition on Eretzara claiming descent from the original Emperor. Their primary goals are to prevent outsiders from claiming the Emperor’s crown and to place one of their own candidates on it. Their influence has made large parts of Eretzara hostile to non-humans and distrustful of outsiders.

---

## The Living Ley

A group of Druids, Elementals, and nature-aligned beings who believe the planetary core is conscious and that its desires are being sabotaged by major builders and leyline transportation systems. They have carried out numerous sabotage attempts against leyline transport hubs.
