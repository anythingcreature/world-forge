# Races & Factions

The world contains a wide variety of sentient peoples and powerful organizations. Many races are capable of magical evolution or transformation, and the boundaries between species are less rigid than in many other worlds.

This section is divided into two main parts:

- **[Sentient Races](/races/sentient-races)** — The major peoples of the world, from the common to the rare and dangerous.
- **[Major Factions](/races/major-factions)** — Organizations and powers whose influence reaches across continents and layers.
