# Sentient Races

Sentient races are those who are intelligent at birth or shortly thereafter. The world contains both familiar peoples and many stranger ones. Magical evolution, dungeon influence, and deliberate transformation mean that the line between races is often blurred.

Below are the races with the greatest current impact on the world.

---

## Humans

The most numerous and widespread race. Their short natural lifespan (roughly 100 years) and high adaptability have led to enormous cultural and physical variety.

Humans hold stakes in nearly every civilized region. They are often excellent negotiators because few other races hold deep racial hatred toward them as a whole. Their curiosity and ability to learn almost any skill is notable, though their short lives mean they rarely reach the absolute peaks of any single discipline while remaining fully human.

Humans have a strong tendency toward change and conflict among themselves. The last time they attempted large-scale unification, they conquered five continents before the emperor died; they are still fighting over his crown.

---

## Elves

Long-lived, rare, and deeply tied to nature and Aether. Elves possess nearly unlimited natural lifespans and exceptional magical affinity. They are the source of many of the greatest advances in magic and the accumulation of knowledge.

Most Elves remain within their forests and maintain strict control over who may enter. When an adult Elf leaves the forest, it is almost always a sign that something significant is occurring. Their culture values control, precision, and understatement; loud or reckless behavior is viewed as a lack of capability.

Young Elves are expected to leave the forest and accomplish something of note before being considered full adults. Those who fail or become unsuitable for Elven society often join the **Dark Elves**, who form the core of the Assassin’s Guild and reject the careful restraint of the forest.

---

## Dwarves

Known for durability, craftsmanship, and the creation of lasting works. Dwarves have a natural lifespan of 500–600 years and a strong disposition toward cultivation. Many live well beyond a thousand years.

Reputation and integrity are central to Dwarven culture. A known liar will find few opportunities. Dwarves maintain a long-standing mutual hostility with Elves, largely driven by competition over resources and differing approaches to problems.

Dwarven strongholds are ruled by Councils of Masters. Challenging a sitting Master is both a path to advancement and a serious social risk if the challenge fails.

---

## Beastkin

A range of peoples existing between human and animal forms. They possess strong affinities for druidry and transformation. Average lifespan is around 300 years, though this varies significantly by type.

Beastkin society is usually tribal and follows the laws of the wild — strength and the ability to hold the loyalty of the tribe matter more than simple combat power. They are often excellent explorers and travel companions, but young Beastkin can struggle to control their instincts and transformations until properly trained.

---

## Halflings

Peoples of mixed ancestry. Appearance and abilities vary widely depending on their lineage. Many display “ancestral markers” — physical traits that reveal their heritage. The more prominent the markers, the stronger the associated affinities tend to be.

Halflings often have an unusual attunement to chance and luck. Their settlements are highly adaptable and can sometimes literally relocate. Pure Halflings (those who have balanced their ancestry to the point of losing obvious markers) are generally accepted among other races.

---

## Antinium

Humanoid ants, roughly human-sized in their most commonly seen forms. Originally a single force under the Grand Queen in the Demon-lands, locked in eternal war with the Fleshweaver.

A portion of the Antinium successfully crossed the ocean and established colonies elsewhere. These became the **Free Antinium**, who no longer answer to the Grand Queen and have lost much of the knowledge required to create new types (including queens). They are widely regarded as a threat; most nations maintain open bounties on Antinium soldiers and especially queens.

Most Antinium function as a hive mind. Independent individuals are rare and fall into categories such as Designed, Individualised, or Aberration.

---

## Demi-monsters

Individuals whose forms have been partially transformed through ritual or prolonged exposure to a dungeon. They are uncommon outside of dungeon-towns and often face social stigma.

Notable types include demi-drakes, faelings, tieflings, and various shifters. They frequently possess advantages drawn from two different natures, but they also absorb Aether more readily and are prone to Aetherwarping if they remain away from a stabilizing influence for too long.

---

## Other Notable Groups

- **Demons** — Extrdimensional entities. Highly varied and rarely stable outside the Demon-lands.
- **Deep Dwellers** — Beings that have lived most of their lives in the high-mana environments of the deeper caverns and Dungeon Layer. Often extremely powerful and dependent on dense mana.
- **Constructs** — Machines, Golems, Elementals, and the rare Synthoids. Created rather than born.
- **Magic-born** — Pure manifestations of mana that form in extreme magical environments. Powerful but short-lived and alien in mindset.
