# Dungeons

Dungeons are one of the most important and dangerous features of the world. To some they are a threat, to others a resource, and to a particular type of person they are an opportunity.

A dungeon begins as the core of a sufficiently powerful being that has died. When enough Potential gathers, the core can develop a form of life and begin influencing the world around it.

Dungeons provide three essential services to the world:

- They create stable monsters that collect corruption and help purge it.
- They actively circulate mana, preventing dangerous uncontrolled magical manifestations.
- They are the primary source of magical items of every variety.

Because of these services, the Adventurer’s Guild generally forbids the destruction of dungeons that are not an active threat to the surrounding area. The immense value of a dungeon core, however, remains a constant temptation.
