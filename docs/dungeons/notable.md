# Notable Dungeons

Certain dungeons have grown powerful, unique, or influential enough to shape entire regions of the world. Below are some of the most significant currently recorded.

---

## The Quantifier

A logic- and potential-attuned dungeon that has spread its influence across an entire continent. It is obsessed with collecting data.

Anyone entering its territory is eventually quantified. Refusal only delays the process; forceful quantification and a binding contract follow. Progress within the Quantifier’s system is measured and directed, giving rise to a civilization built around “levels.”

Approaching the core binds an individual’s potential and fate into the service of the dungeon. This has created an “admin” faction that enforces contracts and wields significant power over other quantified beings.

The Quantifier is unusually tolerant of other dungeons existing within its territory, provided they submit to quantification. It is also the source of the most effective potential-manipulation items in the world, including stat sheets, resetters, potential transfers, and warpers.

---

## The Chaos

The only known dungeon attuned to **Corruption**. Located beneath the center of the Demon-lands.

It is impossible to enter without becoming Aether-warped. The few items recovered from it have seeded cults and created zones of lasting corruption, warped creatures, and dimensional instability. Both the dungeon’s aura and its products tend to bend the minds of those exposed toward spreading further corruption.

Extremely dangerous. Approach is not recommended.

---

## The Digital Dungeon

A unique dungeon created when the Divinity of Technology forced a core to attune to Technology mana. Because of this, the dungeon cannot absorb Aether normally and is entirely reliant on the Divinity — which in turn has become trapped inside it.

The Digital Dungeon exists as an impossible piece of technology capable of holding unlimited information. Using it requires a sacrifice, often greater than most are willing to pay.

It currently powers the Burrowing City. The individual who last activated it remains trapped, existing as a digital construct dedicated to fulfilling the wishes of whoever controls the dungeon’s physical anchor, until the dungeon is activated again or broken open by technology that has reached the limits of existence.

---

## The Path of Eternity

A time-attuned dungeon. From the outside, time within it appears not to pass at all.

When a delver enters, the dungeon creates a new path. Later visitors can see images of previous delvers taking their second step — windows into the past rather than true simultaneous existence.

The dungeon proclaims a reward of immortality to any who complete it. The truth is more complex: long ago an Aether-warped beast of continent-destroying power was imprisoned in a cage of time. Walking the Path joins a battle that has both already ended and not yet begun. Those granted “immortality” are only released after the last participant has joined the fight. If the beast ever wins and consumes the core, it will become an autonomous time-eating entity.

---

## The Origin

Located directly beneath the center of the Uncharted Lands. It is believed to be one of the greatest sources of the variety found in animals and monsters across that continent.

The Origin forces new parties onto fresh paths so that each delve serves as evolutionary inspiration. Repeated delves do not revisit the same location in succession, even if they begin with the same creature type. After periods of intense activity the local ecosystem is often devastated and must recover while the monsters feed on the dungeon’s mana.

---

## The Ascension

Not a true dungeon in the conventional sense, but a region of chaotic and overlapping mana attunements created by the deaths of many high-magic individuals. The area spontaneously generates and destroys high-value treasures and lethal magical manifestations.

Near the center exists a small pocket of relative calm maintained by a young dungeon core struggling to survive. The core’s influence slightly stabilizes the immediate surroundings. If it endures long enough, it may eventually establish a proper dungeon rather than spending all its resources simply preventing its own destruction.

---

## The Trials of the Soul

The largest known collection of trials created by the Soulbinders. These are not a single dungeon but a complex of gates and challenges designed to test worthiness for soul-bound items.

Trials are classified as:

- **Solved** — Beaten and publicly understood. Used for speedrunning and proving competence.
- **Unsolved** — Newly opened or not yet beaten. High risk, high reward.
- **Legacy** — Rare trials designed to select apprentices for the Soulbinders themselves. These test personality and character as well as power.

Scouts specialize in entering trials and returning with information rather than completing them. This information is extremely valuable.
