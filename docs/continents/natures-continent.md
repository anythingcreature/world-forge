# Nature’s Continent

An untamed continent of the wild — not due to a lack of effort. On this continent lies the sleeping goddess of nature. With each breath, the frontier between nature and civilization is pressed back toward the shores.

Between that and the Elves defending its shores, there is no practical way to colonize it. There are countless natural treasures of great value lying within, but also untold perils unknown by any man.

---

## Landmarks

### Goddess Peak

This mountain is the rumored resting place of the God of Nature. There are constant crystals of power and monsters of immense danger appearing, but the treasure is worth it in the eyes of most explorers.

However, in order to even step foot on the mountain, the elves extract an **Aether-bound oath** from you that you will not enter the cave at the peak of the mountain.

Rumors say the last individual who broke this oath returned to the city that once stood on the continent and cast a spell using his own life as a component. This spell wiped out all sentient life other than devotees of nature, reduced the city to dust, and planted thousands of seeds that grew to full maturity in a few seconds.

Ever since, entering the cave has been severely off-limits.
