# Nullands

This part of the World is relatively isolated. There are few dungeons there, but they all specialise in **null mana**. There are also a few leylines, but almost all of them are null mana as well. This leads the entire continent to have almost exclusively null mana or no mana affinity.

This is the safest area of the planet, but anyone who uses mana in almost any capacity avoids it at all costs because it will nullify all magical effects.

Most individuals not from the Nullands can’t stay there for long, as their systems have grown to rely upon mana. The passive absorption of null mana will empty their reservoirs fast. This leads many who have never left the Nullands to believe that magic is merely a myth.

Despite not having mana, the residents have accomplished many great leaps in technology. One of the main inhibitors of the technology they call “computers” is the fact that they generate a small amount of lightning-attuned mana, which will “short-circuit” many of their strange devices. The constant oppressive presence of null mana has enabled their technologies, but this makes the Nullanders apprehensive to leave their continent.

### Notable Features

- There are no cities of any significance.
- The only landmarks of real value are:
  - The Null mana Dungeons
  - The null mana leyline
  - The random spatial leyline

The Null mana Dungeons produce artefacts that, when attached to one of the mystical “Technological” artefacts of the Nullands, will enable them to operate in areas with environmental mana for a period of time. The residents often refer to these dungeons as “military bases” and “factories,” but they clearly have no understanding of the Dungeon cores running the places.

The null mana leyline produces mana crystals that will interrupt any spell or magical effect, but they are very hard to transport.

The spatial leyline is responsible for the majority of travelers that end up in the Nullands. Unfortunately, due to the constant Null mana presence, the moments when the leyline exists are random and very short-lived.

### Warnings

- The aura of Null mana reaches several kilometers into the ocean. Do not approach the Nullands in any craft that has any reliance on mana.
- Leave all magical artefacts, items, cores, and runes behind. Many of them are prone to exploding when the null mana interferes with their operation.
