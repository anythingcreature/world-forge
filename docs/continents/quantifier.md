# Quantifier

This is the continent under the control of the **Quantifier** dungeon. It has a variety of significant areas. It is not recommended to visit unless you have already paid off the debt of Quantitization.

Upon a non-quantized person entering the Quantifier’s lands, they will receive the offer to be quantified. If they do not accept, they have only a limited amount of time before they will be forcefully quantified and made to sign a contract to pay off the debt before leaving again.

---

## Landmarks

### The Pinnacle

The Pinnacle is the location at which people compete to gain one of several titles. It occurs at the top of a mountain surrounded by dangers of all types. This is intentional — it forces each challenger to be able to handle difficulties of every kind.

Once an individual has managed to reach the Pinnacle, they may declare a challenge against any existing title-holder. They then compete in a challenge, viewable by any Quantized individual, to determine who will continue to hold the title.

A title-holder receives many benefits within the system set up by the Quantifier. The title is actually a contract that enables the holder to pull on the potential of the Quantifier, avoiding the potential loss of age. It also makes them an adviser of the Quantifier, giving them the ability to influence it.

However, the Quantifier insists on new data and new advisers. If nobody has beaten a title holder after a period of time and they are no longer providing interesting data, they will be killed by the Quantifier and a tournament for the position will be held.

## Cities

### The Archive

The Archive is a record of all the information gathered by the Quantifier. This information is primarily stored in the form of crystals that simulate an artificial mind. This enables any individual with a sufficient mental affinity to search through the Archive and retrieve the information they need.

The Archive is also filled with items of extreme interest to the Quantifier. These include:

- Items of immense power
- Evolving items (including soul-bound items)
- Items that do not quite Quantify properly

The Archive is a city on the surface filled with individuals trusted by the Quantifier, as well as golems piloted by the Quantifier known as **Non-individual Piloted Constructs** (NPCs).
