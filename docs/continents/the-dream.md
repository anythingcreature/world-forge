# The Dream

The Dream is a land of constantly shifting reality. Very few places within it remain constant. It exists because of the mixing of a leyline of **Creation** with a leyline of **Aetheric mana**.

The Dream can be safely entered and left, but only based upon one’s link to the Aetheric connection. The connection to the Aether stabilizes the area directly around a person as they force their view of the world onto the Aether-infused matter. This makes the region popular with monks, who become nearly impossible to find, and whose own potential controls who can visit them.

For those with a weak Aetheric connection, the Dream is extraordinarily dangerous. They will only be able to hold their own body consistent. This causes them to be shoved around by the shifting currents of the Aether. Many times they will begin absorbing the Aether in an attempt to stabilize it, leading them to become **Aether-warped**. Unlike normal Aetherwarped individuals, those who remain in the Dream can maintain some control while they stay inside it.

## Landmarks

### The Living Mountain

The Living Mountain was once an earth magician who became Aetherwarped. It stabilizes the area around it and serves as a refuge to all who can find it.

In order to maintain the mind of the Living Mountain, a sect of monks is dedicated to etching anti-corruption runes into the mountain, along with runes that keep its mind asleep. If it were to die or awaken, tens of thousands would die.

### The Mount of Ascension

Ascension requires a strong connection to the Aether, so people often come here to attempt it. Whether they succeed or fail, it leaves a permanent imprint on this area of the Aether.

While the area is not constant, there are manifestations of those who have attempted ascension. Those who can stabilize the area can learn from the imprints left by the Ascended.

### The Aetherwell

The Aetherwell is the home of the Master of Aether. She exists in an evershifting form, but any who make their way to her are promised a wish.

Due to the shifting nature of reality — especially in her proximity — almost any wish can be fulfilled. However, this leads to the inevitable Aetherwarping of the recipient. If you are willing to sacrifice your humanity to accomplish your goal, this is one of the easiest ways.

## Cities

### The Monk’s Temple

The only city of note is the Monk’s Temple. They guard the way to the Aetherwell and practice direct Aether manipulation, enabling them to keep the city constant.

The Temple is always moving. Somehow, no matter what direction you approach the Aetherwell from, the Temple must always be passed through.
