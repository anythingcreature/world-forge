# Continents

The Surface is divided into a number of major continents and regions, each shaped by unique combinations of leylines, history, and powerful entities. Some are relatively stable. Others are actively hostile to most forms of life.

Below is an overview of the major landmasses and regions currently recorded.

---

### [Nullands](/continents/nullands)
An isolated continent saturated with null mana. Magic is almost completely suppressed. The inhabitants have developed advanced non-magical technology and largely believe magic to be a myth. Extremely dangerous to any mana-dependent being.

### [Quantifier](/continents/quantifier)
A continent entirely under the influence of the Quantifier dungeon. Visitors are quantified and bound by contracts of potential. Home to the Archive and the Pinnacle, where titles are contested.

### [Demon-lands](/continents/demon-lands)
Centered on a massive dimensional fissure that constantly releases extraplanar entities. Dominated by the Fleshweaver, the Supreme Mimic, and the Antinium Hives. One of the most dangerous regions on the Surface.

### [Frosted Lands](/continents/frosted-lands)
A northern continent locked in extreme cold. Home to the Frozen Citadel and an Elemental of Eternal Ice. Filled with preserved relics and creatures from ancient times.

### [Eretzara](/continents/eretzara)
A human-dominated continent of constant warfare and political intrigue between numerous kingdoms. A center of trade, mercenary work, and relic collection.

### [Nature’s Continent](/continents/natures-continent)
An untamed land protected by the sleeping goddess of nature and the Elves. Civilization is actively pushed back. Home to Goddess Peak and immense natural treasures.

### [Elemental Lands](/continents/elemental-lands)
A region of constantly shifting elemental leylines. Landscapes and local lifeforms change with little warning. Home to the Knowledge Elementals, the Control Center, and the four Elemental Mountains.

### [The Dream](/continents/the-dream)
A land of shifting reality created by the mixing of Creation and Aetheric leylines. Stability depends on one’s connection to the Aether. Home to the Living Mountain, the Mount of Ascension, and the Aetherwell.

### [The Mindscape](/continents/mindscape)
A planar intersection where the subconscious minds of living beings manifest and interact. Extremely dangerous to the unprepared. Home to the City of the Grand Machine.

### [Undersea Alliance](/continents/undersea)
The collective kingdoms of the native ocean-dwelling peoples, along with the air-breathing city-state of Atlantis. Dominated by the threat of the Leviathan and the Aquilin Scar.

### [Uncharted Lands](/continents/uncharted-lands)
A continent warped by intense Spatial mana. Terrain constantly shifts, making conventional mapping impossible. Home to the Spatial Spire, the Outsider’s Home, and the Edge of the World.
