# Uncharted Lands

Exactly what the name implies — lands that nobody has successfully mapped. This is not due to a lack of effort, but because the terrain is always shifting. The prevalence of **Spatial mana** on this continent makes navigation nearly impossible, and the landscape constantly rearranges itself.

Exploration crews will often walk a few hundred feet into a forest, only to turn around and find themselves staring at a desert or a mountain where ocean existed moments before.

---

## Landmarks

### The Spatial Spire

The primary Spatial Magic research facility in the world. It lies at the center of the continent. Due to its constantly maintained enchantments, it remains consistent in location. The path to it is always straight, but the land it passes through changes often and quickly.

## Notable Locations

### Outsider’s Home

A place for all those cast out from society. The main population consists of Halflings, but they allow anyone willing to follow village rules. No one knows exactly how many villages exist. They have almost become a trading empire within the Uncharted Lands — almost, because while they trade among themselves regularly, they have little experience communicating when different villages are teleported near one another.

Other races have attempted to colonize the Uncharted Lands, but very few survive the random teleportations, and those who do usually lose contact with the outside world. The Halflings, due to their adaptability, have managed to thrive.

There are two main types of villages:

- **Raiders** — Outcasts who refuse to be tied down. They function more like traveling bands of 30–40 people.
- **Traders** — The majority of the population. They focus on trade and adaptation.

Every now and then a village will become waterborne and drift away. When this happens, the village will either seek other Halflings to bring back, or attempt to return to the mainland.

### The Edge of the World

The Edge of the World is not actually the edge. It is a circle of ocean approximately 100 kilometers in circumference filled with an immense amount of structured spatial magic.

For every ten kilometers you travel inward (relative to your size), your size halves — and so does the size of everything around you. This creates the illusion of continuing on forever.

The endless ocean contained within is home to living islands and a vast number of spatially aligned creatures, forming an ever-shifting maze. A few civilizations exist here that do not even know the outside world exists.

It is rumored that at the center of this circle lies the inheritance of the old Ascended of Space. Not even the current Ascended of Space has managed to reach the center, despite many attempts.
