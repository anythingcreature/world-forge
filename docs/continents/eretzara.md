# Eretzara

A continent as diverse in landscape as the populous that lives thereon. While the human kingdoms own every inch of its surface, there is constant trade and war between them.

This makes it the ideal location for any seeking to profit off of the endless conflicts between the kingdoms. It is also the place to go if you have any interesting relics to sell — there are always collectors.

---

## Landmarks

The most relevant landmarks are the many capital cities of the kingdoms. However, their relative importance varies too much to be consistent.
