# Elemental Lands

The Elemental Lands are characterized by large leylines of pure elemental energy that pool their mana on the surface. This leads to large stretches of land that are attuned to only one element, and many animals that have gained the ability to change their attunement to match their surroundings.

The major issue with the Elemental Lands is that these leylines do not always remain constant — they can switch at a moment’s notice. What was once a lush forest can quickly shift into a desert or a swamp with little to no warning. This unique problem makes the region suitable only for those who are prepared for everything.

The most relevant sentients in the Elemental Lands are the **Knowledge Elementals**. They are not quite normal sentients. They have built a giant machine near the center of the continent that can predict and manipulate the elemental shifts. They use this to keep anyone they dislike away.

The only other sentients are a strange race of **shifters**. They change with the land, and so do their cities. This makes them a major obstacle, because as their element shifts, so does everything about them. They can change from agreeable traders to raging barbarians in seconds, with only a few minutes of warning.

The Elemental Lands are only for the adaptable. Those who can adapt to the quickly changing landscape and enemies will gain large amounts of knowledge and treasure. Those who cannot change their ways will find themselves destroyed, consumed, or absorbed into the constantly changing world around them.

## Landmarks

### The Elemental Mountains

The Elemental Mountains are four large peaks. There is one at each cardinal direction from the center, and each is constantly attuned to one of the four basic elements. These mountains do not have the usual adaptable animals — instead they have creatures that are extraordinarily adapted to one specific environment and element.

One unique feature of these mountains is that each emits its own gravitational field. This makes the slopes more shallow than expected and building on them nearly impossible.

#### Mountain of Fire (South)
A volcano that is constantly leaking lava, with many geysers of flame shooting out of cracks along its side. Despite how dangerous this sounds, it is both the safest and smallest of the mountains. The non-physical nature of fire prevents the mountain from growing on its own, which limits the concentration of mana.

#### Mountain of Earth (East)
A large mountain that appears to have no plants. In reality, many of the plants there have petrifying spores that can turn a person to stone. There are also large crystals under extreme pressure — some will explode at the lightest touch and spray shards that shred and pin creatures. These shards then begin growing into more crystals. The creatures here often have durable crystal exoskeletons.

#### Mountain of Water (North)
Filled with mind-bending structures where stone flows like water and water behaves like stone. Animals here can swim through stone as if it were water. The greatest danger is the random tidal waves that form at the peak and cascade down the entire mountain, filled with deadly shards of stone and ice.

#### Mountain of Air (West)
This mountain floats a few hundred meters above the surface and occasionally falls, crushing everything beneath it. It is not solid — it is a chain of floating chunks of mountain reaching all the way to the top of the atmosphere. These chunks are constantly moving, colliding, and falling. Anyone not actively touching one of the crystals found on the mountain will be targeted by the wind and stripped of their possessions or thrown through the mountain. The creatures here are not flying creatures — they focus on jumping at extreme speeds and launching projectiles even faster.

### The Control Center

The Control Center is one of the few known magitech computers. It can operate because it exists right in the middle of a logic leyline. This makes it the ideal location for Knowledge Elementals to live, and the only place where a structure like this could be possible.

The Control Center can analyse and manipulate the network of leylines beneath the Elemental Lands. Some believe the leylines of the Elemental Lands are fully controlled by the Control Center and that the evolution of the landscape is monitored and studied by the Knowledge Elementals. They are correct — but most people do not believe it.

The Control Center holds the most information on leylines and mana combinations in the known world. They also study elemental manifestations, particularly when a life leyline is channeled alongside another leyline.

## Cities & Tribes

There are currently no permanent cities in the Elemental Lands. In the past, some groups struck deals with the Knowledge Elementals to build temporary towns. Most of these were destroyed when the people broke the deal. A few finished their goals and left, or became part of the land itself. These are among the groups that founded the shifter tribes found deep in the Elemental Lands.

The tribes of the Elemental Lands are nomads, farmers, blacksmiths, traders, hunters, and city dwellers all at once. They shift with the land around them and are deeply in tune with the leylines. This allows them to create a city in a short time, live there for a week or two, and pack up and leave just before the land transforms — or simply adapt to live in whatever the land has become.
