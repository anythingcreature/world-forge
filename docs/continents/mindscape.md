# The Mindscape

The Mindscape is the physical location where someone can move from physical space into the mental plane. The Mindscape is a plane of existence where the subconscious minds of people connect and war against each other.

Proficient mind mages can manifest there temporarily. For the average person, the only way to temporarily pass into the landscape is by using this planar intersection.

There are three different ways that people’s minds manifest in the undefined areas of the Mindscape:

1. **Kingdom of Mind** — The most powerful form of mental manifestation. Very few people have the mental strength to create one, and they can only be made by those actually living in the Mindscape. These kingdoms can sustain individuals of pure mental power as manifestations of one’s subconscious, field armies of mental constructs, and even sustain real individuals as residents.

2. **Sphere of Thought** — Usually the manifestation of a dream. These float around the Mindscape and can be entered in order to view the dream and interact with the dreamer.

3. **Image of Self** — Usually just a small spark. When someone is stressed or empowered with mental magic, it expands temporarily and organizes the landscape of inactive minds around it.

There are no permanent residents in the conventional sense, but those with powerful enough minds can create anything — though it will only be sustained as long as the person is actively maintaining the manifestation.

---

## Entering the Mindscape

When entering the Mindscape, a person will form a kingdom or walk on the surface. As their mind grows tired, they will be pulled toward a dream or an image of self.

- Touching a **dream** pulls you in. You must survive the dream and find a way out, or travel from dream to dream using dream bridges.
- Touching an **image of self** begins a battle of willpower. The loser is knocked out, and the winner drains mental strength from the loser to supplement their own.

**Warning:** If you fall asleep in the Mindscape, you will form either a Dream bubble or a Kingdom of Mind. You cannot escape your own dream bubble alone — it is nearly impossible without help from a mental mage. Those who fall into their own dream bubble physically almost always die inside their own dream.

---

## Navigation

There is no consistent landmark for navigating the Mindscape. Strong mental mages can route a path from one location to another, but ultimately there is no reliable method other than relying on a person with a strong mind or a skilled Mind mage.

Dreams floating around are typically close to people with whom one has a strong relationship, though these groupings are not always reliable. For most people, it is safer and quicker to navigate through the dreams than to attempt walking the Mindscape between them.

---

## Cities

There is one consistent Kingdom of Mind: the mind of the **Grand Machine**. This is currently the only proven existence of the Grand Machine.

The city runs on a tight schedule, but the Grand Machine does not mind visitors. It often takes inspiration from them and will give them a chance to refill their mental power before continuing on.

The City of the Grand Machine travels around the Mindscape attempting to save those trapped in their own dream bubbles. However, there are almost no mental mages powerful enough to risk such work.
