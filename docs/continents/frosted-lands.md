# Frosted Lands

A land to the North that is very inhospitable to any lifeform. Rumors speak of an ancient civilisation that did everything they could to freeze time. There are many relics and monsters from long ago preserved and trapped in the ice.

The most dangerous substance found here is **eternal ice**. If touched, it will freeze almost anything instantly.

---

## Landmarks

### The Frozen Citadel

This citadel is the only structure made by intelligent life on the entire continent. It is ruled over by an Elemental of Eternal Ice.

Once a man, he is now only a manifestation and a preservation of what he once was — sealed unchanging by the eternal ice he consumed. He rules the citadel, where he creates soldiers from the very ice that surrounds him.

These soldiers march forth, creating watchposts, forts, and barracks everywhere. They are hostile to all detectable life.
