# The Undersea Alliance

The Undersea Alliance consists of all the kingdoms of native ocean dwellers. These kingdoms are united due to the many dangers that constantly assail them.

They are not particularly advanced technologically or magically, but they are the only force that has instinctual three-dimensional battle patterns. This makes them highly effective at fighting in the water near their home. The less familiar they are with the terrain, the less effective these strategies become.

---

## Landmarks

### Aquilin Scar / Captain’s Grave

To those living on the land, this is a place where only those who desire to risk their lives (and their crews’) dare to go. Very few return, and even those who do are usually majorly injured for life. Land-dwellers call it the **Captain’s Grave**.

Aquilin Scar is a giant trench where the city of Aquilin once stood. It was created 600 years ago. A great serpent now lives there. No one knows how deep the trench truly is, but rumor claims it reaches all the way down to the dungeon layer.

Many of the sea folk believe it was the act of a God or Goddess, though no one knows why the city was destroyed. Because of their beliefs, they sacrifice captured sailors twice a year to the Trench in an attempt to appease the gods and prevent it from happening again.

This is the home of the **Leviathan** — a wyrm that lives off the abundant supply of water mana and was responsible for the destruction of Aquilin.

The Leviathan constantly seeks to grow its domain. It uses its affinity for water to cause major storms above the trench and continuously tries to make the trench deeper and wider. Due to the shifting nature of the caverns and dungeon layer, this effort is somewhat futile, but it has kept Aquilin Scar in existence for hundreds of years.

## Cities

### Atlantis

Atlantis is a city-state founded by air-breathers who chose to live under the sea. They have created advanced bio-machines that enable undersea travel and combat. This is the main strength of their civilization: the ability to create creatures to accomplish whatever they need.

However, the creatures they create are short-lived (1–2 year lifespans), and the city has already reached the maximum size it can sustainably support.

They specialize in druids who bond with bioengineered familiars. This produces elite undersea travelers and adventurers. They are the only nation with air-breathing underwater units, making them essential for preventing monster overload from undersea dungeons.

Occasionally they suggest sending a group of explorers out to found another city, but they lack the necessary supplies and are constantly fighting off parasites that infect their bioengineered air domes.
