# Demon-lands

The Demon-lands are the lands held by interdimensional interlopers, also known as the **demons**. At the center of the continent is a massive stable dimensional fissure that constantly cycles dimensions, dumping hordes of other-dimensional entities onto the continent to attune to the world and to fight for their lives.

This constant supply of new life leads to many strange and powerful life-forms that rely on the constant ability to hunt and to consume other beings to live. When one of them escapes the Demon-lands, they become a problem that usually requires a global response.

---

## Landmarks

### The Dimensional Fissure

At the center of the Demon-lands lies a semi-permanent, semi-stable fissure between dimensions. It originated from a failed experiment to form a stable portal to the core of the world. This fissure cycles between foreign dimensions and concentrations of life in those worlds. This is what causes the massive amount of foreign and powerful entities in the Demon-lands.

Directly around the Dimensional Fissure lie three major powers:

- The Fleshweaver
- The Supreme Mimic
- The Antinium

Between each of these factions lies a narrow path through which new arrivals can attempt to escape without being consumed or destroyed.

### The Fleshweaver

Just as much a feature of the land as a living being, the Fleshweaver takes the constant flow of new living creatures and consumes them while turning them into massive, powerful, but ultimately flawed creatures. (See Unique Entities for more.)

### The Supreme Mimic

The Supreme Mimic is a Mimic not interested in moving at all. It simply consumes everything that comes its way and replicates whatever interests it. It has a living and thriving city on its back. The only reason the city has not yet been consumed is because they have a deal with the Supreme Mimic… and it finds them cute. (See Unique Entities for more.)

### The Antinium Hives

The Antinium Hives are the origin of the Antinium as a race. They are one of the Fleshweaver’s creations. However, they remained intelligent, stable, and retained the ability to reproduce — making them a very rare exception. (See Races for more.)

Within the Antinium Hives there are three primary goals:

1. Absolute efficiency
2. Constant progress
3. Stopping the Fleshweaver

This leads to a constant war on the surface and extending deep into the caverns.

The Antinium are ruled by the **Grand Queen** — the only Antinium Queen authorized to make new types of Antinium and the supreme monarch of the Antinium. Under her are many queens, each specialized in creating certain types of Antinium and keeping the hives running.

## Cities

### Replicanus

This is the only city on the Demon Continent. They claim to have tamed the Supreme Mimic. The only reason they survive the constant onslaught by the wild monsters, the Fleshweaver’s mutants, and the Antinium’s attacks is because the Supreme Mimic likes keeping them around.

That is not an insult to their strength. They have many experts of their own and would be a significant power on any other continent. However, it is unknown how many of those experts or residents are simply the Supreme Mimic having a bit of fun.
