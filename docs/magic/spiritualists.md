# Spiritualists

Spiritualists align themselves with forces they consider “natural.” They tend to be highly mystical in their approach and view magic as something that should not be spoiled by the unworthy.

Most Spiritualists prefer to remain apart from larger society. The major exception is the priesthood — when a Priest speaks, society tends to listen, if only out of fear of divine consequences. When a true Druid or Oracle becomes involved in worldly affairs, it is almost always a sign that something significant is about to occur.

This has created a widespread stigma: the appearance of a Spiritualist is often taken as an omen that trouble is imminent. In reality, Spiritualists usually appear because the trouble can no longer be ignored.

---

## Core Method: Spiritual Bonds

Spiritualists gain power by forming contracts with entities or natural forces. These contracts require the Spiritualist to give something up in exchange for power that must be used according to the nature of the contracted force.

The main categories are:

### Priests
Those who dedicate themselves to a higher being. This includes Paladins, Clerics, Warlocks, Cultists, and similar roles.

- **Gods / Devils** → Cultist, Cleric, or Paladin. Power is granted according to how well the individual upholds the ideals of the being. Breaking those ideals brings punishment and eventual loss of power.
- **Ascendants** → Hero. The Hero is required to act according to the Ascendant’s will at certain times or in specific situations. Failure to complete a given quest brings severe consequences.
- **Extraplanar / Eldritch Entities** → Warlock. These beings often do not fully understand the mortal world, resulting in strange rules, disproportionate powers, and unpredictable punishments.

### Druids
Those who bond with natural forces. They typically progress through plant, animal, and land bonds, though many never achieve all three.

- **Animal Druids** gain understanding of (and often the ability to transform into) specific animals. They frequently live with and elevate the intelligence of their chosen species.
- **Plant Druids** develop deep understanding and control over plant life. Their forests are often highly optimized symbiotic systems. Entering a Plant Druid’s laboratory without extreme caution is widely considered suicidal.
- **Land Druids** operate on the scale of the land itself. Their work is often invisible for years or decades. Upon their death, the land may either avenge them or improve itself in their memory.
- **True Druids** refuse to hear only one aspect of nature. They balance the needs of civilization against the needs of the natural world and can be extremely dangerous when that balance is threatened.

### Seers
Those who read the natural paths of Aether in an attempt to understand the will of the world.

- **Farseers** form networks for long-distance communication and can peer through space to places or objects they have a connection with.
- **Oracles** receive visions of possible futures. A true Oracle who has fully sacrificed one of their natural senses can produce prophecies that *will* be fulfilled in one form or another.
- **Communers** can read the history and potential of objects, and in extreme cases can track or influence people through objects strongly connected to them.

### Dungeon-Bound
A special category of Spiritualist who has shifted their natural affinity to the mana of a specific dungeon. This grants the ability to communicate with and guide the dungeon, but rapidly makes the individual dependent on the dungeon’s mana and aura.

Over time, a Dungeon-bound individual’s body, affinities, and personality warp to better match the dungeon’s desires. They always retain at least a fragment of their original self, but prolonged binding eventually turns them into little more than an extension of the dungeon.

Dungeon-bound individuals are the origin of many demi-monster races. Children who leave a dungeon early enough can sometimes develop normal Aether and mana channels, allowing them to live outside — though returning later is usually catastrophic.
