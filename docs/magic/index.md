# Magic Systems

Magic in this world is not a single practice. It is a collection of fundamentally different approaches to the same underlying forces: **Aether**, **Mana**, and **Potential**.

Over time, these approaches have crystallized into distinct traditions, each with its own philosophy, methods, strengths, and dangers:

- **[Aether, Mana & Potential](/magic/aether-mana-potential)** — The foundational energies of the world and how they interact.
- **[Cultivators](/magic/cultivators)** — Those who internalize mana, refine it into Qi, and reshape their bodies and lifespans.
- **[Mages](/magic/mages)** — Those who externalize mana through spellforms, runes, and foci.
- **[Alchemists](/magic/alchemists)** — Those who treat magic as a natural science to be studied, codified, and engineered.
- **[Spiritualists](/magic/spiritualists)** — Those who form contracts with natural forces, gods, spirits, or dungeons.
- **[Attunements](/magic/attunements)** — The system of elemental and conceptual affinities that underlies almost all magical practice.
