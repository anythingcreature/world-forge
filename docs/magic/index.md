# Magic Systems

Magic in this world is built on a shared foundation of energies and affinities. Different groups have developed distinct traditions for working with those forces, each with its own philosophy, methods, and risks.

---

## Underlying Mechanics

These are the fundamental forces and systems that all magical practice depends on:

- **[Aether, Mana & Potential](/magic/aether-mana-potential)** — The three principal energies of the world and how they transform into one another.
- **[Attunements](/magic/attunements)** — The system of elemental and conceptual affinities that determines what kinds of magic an individual can wield and how strongly.

---

## Major Traditions

Over time, several major approaches to using magic have developed. These are not completely separate systems — they are different philosophies and methods for working with the same underlying forces.

- **[Cultivators](/magic/cultivators)** — Internalize mana, refine it into Qi, and reshape the body and lifespan. Focus on personal power and longevity.
- **[Mages](/magic/mages)** — Externalize mana through spellforms, runes, and foci. Focus on control, versatility, and structured spellcraft.
- **[Alchemists](/magic/alchemists)** — Treat magic as a natural science to be studied, codified, and engineered. Focus on knowledge, items, and large-scale effects.
- **[Spiritualists](/magic/spiritualists)** — Form contracts with natural forces, gods, spirits, or dungeons. Focus on relationships, bargains, and borrowed power.
