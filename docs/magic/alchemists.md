# Alchemists

Alchemists treat magic as another side of natural science. By studying the phenomena produced by mana, they have developed runes, rituals, and a wide range of practical applications. They constantly contest the Mages’ claims over runic knowledge.

Alchemists generally find Cultivators fascinating but largely irrelevant to their work. They tend to dislike Spiritualists, viewing them as religious mystics who refuse to examine reality clearly. Relations with Mages are often productive — until a major discovery is made, at which point competition frequently destroys the collaboration.

Instead of rigid sects or schools, Alchemists tend to operate as loose networks of individuals with shared interests. Many will temporarily gather to pursue a breakthrough or trade knowledge on the black market.

Alchemists are highly valued by expanding towns and kingdoms because they can reliably produce results. The cost, however, is high. Wise rulers limit experimentation to areas outside their own territory — the disasters are rarely worth the risk when they occur inside the walls.

## Paths of Study

Alchemists usually dedicate themselves to one primary path of study:

### Transmutation
The transformation of materials, creation of potions, and preparation of components. Considered the safest of the major paths, though many alchemists have still died from their own experiments. The traditional peaks of this path include the Elixir of Life, the creation of gold from lead, and the production of alchemical solvent.

### Rituals
Focused on the use of runes, components, and precise natural circumstances to create large-scale magical effects. Divided into three main sub-disciplines:

- **Summoning** — Calling entities from other dimensions or locations
- **Magical Item Creation** — Permanently altering objects through runes and ritual
- **War-spells** — Large cooperative workings capable of leveling cities or reshaping terrain

### Natural Phenomena
These alchemists study the rules that nature follows, then identify the parallel magical rules, and finally apply those rules on a much larger scale. Common specializations include magical architecture, formation work, and leyline manipulation.

### Forbidden Paths
The rarest and most dangerous route. Focused on sacrifice and the deliberate breaking of natural laws. Common methods include:

- Ritualistic sacrifice (generating large quantities of manipulable Aether Shadows)
- Blood magic (amplifying spells with the caster’s own life force at the cost of long-term deterioration)
- Mana cycling (using the residual Aether Shadow left after a spell as if it were fresh mana, rapidly leading to Aether-warping)

## Legacy and Knowledge

Many Alchemists leave behind tomes of knowledge and design trials to test those who seek their secrets. This practice has left numerous ruins across the world that adventurers frequently attempt to raid.

The most valuable of these are **grimoires** — tomes that have been infused with magical potential and gained a form of sentience. A grimoire directly enhances the magical ability of its user, but when unbound it absorbs a portion of that ability in return.

Dark Alchemists represent the worst excesses of the tradition. Unrestricted experimentation, trade with cults, and a complete lack of ethical limits make them a recurring source of plagues, monstrous outbreaks, and corrupted settlements.
