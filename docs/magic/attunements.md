# Attunements

Mana in this world is naturally divided into multiple affinities. These affinities are grouped by rarity and by the part of reality they affect. The vast majority of magic operates through one or more of these attunements.

The major categories are:

- **Elemental**
- **Energy**
- **Attribute**
- **Meta**
- **Primal**
- **Other** (Special)

Most individuals are born with at least minor attunement to several basic elements. Strong natural affinity makes growth in that element significantly easier.

---

## Basic Attunements

### Elemental
The foundational blocks of the physical world. Common and relatively straightforward.

| Element | Domain | Classic Effect | Inverse |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Fire | Heat, flame, transformation | Fireball | Frost |
| Water | Fluids, flow, change | Water Pulse | Salt |
| Earth | Stone, durability, steadfastness | Rock Wall | Dust |
| Air | Motion, freedom, pressure | Gust | Vacuum |

### Energy
Controls non-physical aspects of reality.

| Element | Domain | Classic Effect | Inverse |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Light | Illumination, illusion, truth/deception | Sunbeam | Darkness |
| Sound | Communication, vibration, disruption | Echo Blast | Silence |
| Life | Health, growth, adaptation | Regrowth | Death |
| Motion | Movement, acceleration, change | Launch | Inertia |

### Attribute
Rarer. Deals with the existence of living beings and how they interact with the world.

| Element | Domain | Classic Effect | Inverse |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Body | Flesh, strength, transformation | Adrenaline | Husk / Necrosis |
| Mind | Thought, processing, emotion | Telepathy | Madness |
| Fate | Luck, destiny, probability | Prophecy | Chance |
| Mana | Control, combination, detection | Detect Mana | Null |

### Meta
Manipulates the nature of magic itself. Higher-level affinities in these are uncommon.

| Element | Domain | Classic Effect | Inverse |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Logic | Structure, instruction, runes | Ward | Chaos / Aberration |
| Connection | Bonds, links, interaction | Connect | Severance |
| Amplification | Power, scale | Boost | Dampening |
| Resonance | Harmony, detection, weaving | Detect Magic | Interference |

### Primal
The domain of gods, legends, and fundamental forces. Extremely rare.

| Element | Domain | Classic Effect | Inverse |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------|
| Space | Distance, volume, teleportation | Blink | Void |
| Time | Causality, speed, stasis | Haste | Stasis |
| Syntropy | Order, synergy, concentration | Synergy | Entropy |
| Attraction | Gravity, magnetism, pull | Retrieve | Repulsion |

### Other (Special)
Exceptions that do not follow normal rules.

- **Divine** — Produced by a divinity spending its own energy. Can substitute for other mana types but warps the result toward the divinity’s nature.
- **Dungeon** — The influence and refined experience of a dungeon core. Naturally multi-elemental.
- **Corrupted** — Residual shell left by extradimensional energy. Warps whatever it touches.
- **Creation / Destruction** — True versions of these forces belong to entities beyond this world (Reality Crafters and Nameless Ends). Mortals who briefly touch them tend to burn out rapidly.

## Attunement Ranking

Attunements are measured on a scale that increases exponentially in power:

- **Ranks 1–3**: Passive influence only. Little to no active control.
- **Ranks 4–10**: Active spellcasting and elemental detection become possible.
- **Ranks 11–30**: A passive aura forms that shapes the element according to the user’s will. At the high end this aura rivals expert-level active casting.
- **Ranks 31–50**: The individual begins losing their humanity and embodying the element. At rank 50 they risk fully transforming into an elemental being (or achieving a prismatic soul if balanced across all elements).

When one element pulls significantly ahead of the others (more than one rank higher past rank 30), it can begin consuming the weaker attunements. This leads to rapid loss of humanity and eventual soul damage unless the individual can reach a stable endpoint.

A perfectly balanced set of all twenty primary elements at rank 50 produces a **prismatic soul** — one of the few known paths to true immortality within the system’s normal rules.

---

## Environmental Attunements

Environments themselves carry attunements. At low levels these simply make certain magics easier or harder. At medium levels they produce consistent magical manifestations. At the highest levels, natural laws begin to break down in favor of the dominant elemental rules.
