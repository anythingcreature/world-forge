# Aether, Mana & Potential

The magic of this world rests on three principal forms of energy: **Aether**, **Mana**, and **Potential**. These energies are transformed from one to another by natural processes that have developed over vast spans of time.

---

## Aether

Aether is the energy created as universes grind against each other. It is present in every universe, though the method of manifestation and the quantity vary.

In this world, Aether does not usually appear directly on the physical plane. Instead, it has formed its own plane, known by several names:

- The Sea of Mana
- The Spiritual Plane
- The Aetheric Plane

This Aetheric Plane contains the Aetheric shadows of all existing life and infused material. These shadows can be manipulated, but the effects are often unpredictable and dangerous. Those who have mastered the art of manipulating Aethershadows can influence future growth, though rarely without significant risk.

Aether can be transformed into every known type of energy. Living beings on this plane slowly absorb Aether and convert it into usable forms — primarily Mana and Potential.

Directly channeling Aether instead of Mana is possible, but extremely dangerous. It can destroy an individual in multiple ways. Those who succeed, however, can accomplish nearly anything.

---

## Potential

Living beings generate Potential over time as their Aethershadow absorbs and manifests Aether. As a being ages, the Aethershadow weakens and frays, eventually leading to negative potential generation.

Potential is allocated to different aspects of a being to enhance and expand their capabilities. There are three primary states of Potential:

- **Free Potential** — Unused and unallocated. Allows growth and adaptation.
- **Reserved Potential** — Allocated toward specific attributes (physical, mental, magical, or skills). Enhances growth in those areas.
- **Realized (Fulfilled) Potential** — Potential that has already been achieved and integrated.

When a being kills another, it can absorb some of the victim’s Potential (and, more rarely, portions of their Aethershadow in the form of memories or lifespan).

Losing too much Potential can cause rapid regression of the body, often leading to permanent distortion. Repairing these distortions is extremely difficult and is primarily the domain of the most skilled Potential mages. Many instead risk using a **Warper**, which reforges the Aethershadow into a new (and often unpredictable) form.

---

## The Relationship Between the Three

Aether is the raw cosmic energy.  
Mana is the refined, usable magical energy that most practitioners work with.  
Potential is the capacity for growth, change, and the elevation of a being beyond its natural limits.

Most magical traditions interact primarily with Mana, while a smaller number work more directly with Potential or attempt to touch Aether itself. The deepest and most dangerous practices often involve manipulating the relationships between all three.
