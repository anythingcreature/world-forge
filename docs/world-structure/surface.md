# The Surface

This is the place where the majority of sentients live. It is a relatively peaceful place compared to the other layers. However, it is also the place most prone to extraplanar invasion. This doesn’t happen often, but when it does it often leads to wars spanning continents. There are also a few dimensions that will open portals and let out surges of foreign lifeforms. These, locally known as **monster surges**, often lead to the gathering of rare materials that cannot be formed on this plane.

The unique part of the Surface is the massive variety in environments. This large variety of environments gives a massive number of different species the opportunity to live. This leads to many dangerous zones — not necessarily dangerous due to the species living there, but because what lives there is often difficult, if not impossible, to predict.

In addition, as this is the area with the most variety, this is where deities often reside in physical form. This leads them to warping the environment further than usually possible, and also warping the wildlife. The most well-known areas of this are:

- The Physical Dream
- The Mindscape
- The mana-null zone

This is the area most densely populated of the layers. There are cities found in almost every environment and across every continent. But these cities are just as often as not destroyed, ruins, or monster cities. The large variety of sentient species has led to some cities being unrecognisable to different species.

The biggest pull of the Surface is that it is mostly consistent, and many areas are safe — at least for the natives. This is why the weakest and the newest are found on the Surface. It is where the life of sentients began, and it will always be home to many, even if a home they could never return to. There are many groups dedicated to making sure there are places where anybody will be safe, as well as many hidden civilisations that only the residents are aware of.
