# World Structure

The world is divided into distinct layers, each with its own rules, dangers, and opportunities. From the relatively stable Surface down to the extreme environment of the Core, every layer shapes the lives of those who dwell within it — or attempt to.

- **[The Surface](/world-structure/surface)** — The primary home of most sentient life. Varied, relatively stable, and the most densely populated layer. Also the region most vulnerable to extraplanar invasion.

- **[The Caverns](/world-structure/caverns)** — A shifting buffer zone between the Surface and the Dungeon Layer. Home to traders, retired legends, powerful magical beasts, and many of the spatial leylines used for rapid travel.

- **[The Dungeon Layer](/world-structure/dungeon-layer)** — The deepest commonly accessible layer. Dominated by dungeon cores, extreme magical environments, and constant change. Source of most powerful magical items and monster cores.

- **[The Skylands](/world-structure/skylands)** — Floating islands and archipelagos formed along air leylines. Temporary by nature, sparsely inhabited, and one of the few places where large-scale magical experimentation can be conducted with relative safety.

- **[The Core](/world-structure/core)** — The deepest and most extreme region of the world. Only those who have achieved a prismatic soul can survive here for any meaningful time. The ultimate source of many leylines and a place where the normal rules of magic and matter break down.
