# The Dungeon Layer

The Dungeon Layer is the deepest and most dangerous of the accessible layers of the world. It is a constantly shifting realm dominated by dungeon cores and the environments they create.

This is where the majority of true dungeons form and grow. The layer itself is unstable — its depth and structure change as dungeons expand, collapse, or are destroyed. Because of this, the boundary between the Caverns and the Dungeon Layer is not fixed.

The Dungeon Layer is the primary source of:

- Magical items of every variety
- Monster cores and components
- High concentrations of specialized mana
- Some of the most powerful entities in the world

Most dungeons actively circulate mana and purify corruption. This makes them both a danger and a necessary part of the world’s magical ecosystem. Destroying a dungeon that is not an active threat to the surrounding area is generally discouraged by the Adventurer’s Guild, though the value of a dungeon core remains a strong temptation.

Traveling in the Dungeon Layer without significant preparation is extremely dangerous. The environment can shift without warning, and many areas are dominated by the will of powerful dungeon cores.
