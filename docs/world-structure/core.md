# The Core

The Core is the deepest and most extreme region of the world. It is not a place that can be reached by normal means, and very few beings have ever survived contact with it.

As one descends past the Dungeon Layer, magical pressure and density increase dramatically. Within the Core itself, the concentration of mana becomes so extreme that reality begins to break down under the strain.

### Conditions

- Only those who have achieved a **prismatic soul** (having reached the peak of all twenty elemental affinities in balance) can survive for any meaningful length of time.
- All others are rapidly warped by the extreme mana density, shredded by tides of crystallized mana, or killed outright.
- Even a prismatic soul can only remain for a short time before the environment becomes unsustainable.

### Nature of the Core

The Core is the ultimate source of many of the world’s leylines and the deepest expression of the planet’s magical nature. It is a place of pure, overwhelming power where the normal rules of magic and matter no longer hold in any reliable way.

Very little concrete information exists about the Core. Most knowledge is second-hand, theoretical, or drawn from the brief experiences of the extremely few individuals who have returned.
