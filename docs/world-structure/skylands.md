# The Skylands

An interesting feature of magic in this world is its habit of congregating. The Skylands are the largest expression of this phenomenon.

Near the emergence points of air leylines, and along their paths through the sky, chunks of earth often become air-attuned and take to the sky. This has created archipelagos of floating islands along these pathways. In addition, there are sectors of the sky where a constant stream of material is launched along these routes.

The Skylands are relatively peaceful compared to other layers, but this is largely because almost nothing is permanent. All land that is lifted eventually falls — with the exception of air dungeons, gravity dungeons, lairs of similarly attuned monsters, and grand works of air magic. Each of these exceptions carries its own dangers.

The main threats in the Skylands are the many flocks of flying monsters: Rocs, dragons, air elementals, sentient islands, harpies, mechanical eagles, and more. These range from a nuisance to a fast trip back to the surface (usually leading to or following your death).

The area is very sparsely populated because safe, stable land is nearly nonexistent. Paradoxically, this also makes it one of the safest places to live — provided you can guarantee your island is not about to fall out from beneath you.

The few inhabited areas are rare and often treated as rumor by surface dwellers. There are a small number of flying cities filled with people dedicated to pushing the limits of travel magic. This is also one of the few places where large-scale magical experiments can be conducted with relative safety, as environmental mana decreases the higher one goes, and there are fewer creatures nearby that can be affected by the fallout of a failed experiment.
